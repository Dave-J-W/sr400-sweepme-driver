# docs

Nothing in this folder is loadable by SweepMe!. It is the documentation, the real data it is
checked against, and the record of how the driver came to be.

## For operators

- [THRESHOLD_SCAN_GUIDE.md](THRESHOLD_SCAN_GUIDE.md) — choosing the discriminator threshold,
  step by step, for someone who has just learned what the threshold is; which level suits your
  signal rate (§6); and finding the best PMT high voltage. Illustrated with example figures from a simulated R8486; [`images/`](images/)
  holds those figures and the CSVs they are drawn from, so
  `python ../Logger-Stanford_SR400/selftest.py plot images/<example>.csv` regenerates them.
- [SIMULATED_VS_REAL.md](SIMULATED_VS_REAL.md) — each of the guide's simulated figures beside
  the real scans of the tube, panel by panel: what the simulator gets right, what it gets
  wrong (a noise hump it lacks, a dark tail ten times too high), and what that means when you read
  your own figure.
- [`real_data/2026-11-10/`](real_data/2026-11-10/) — real dark and light scans, raw CSVs exactly
  as measured, the driver's reports from the time, and figures regenerated by the current code.
  Its README says which of the old reports' conclusions have since been corrected.

The driver's own operator reference is its [README](../Logger-Stanford_SR400/README.md):
installing, the GUI, the end-of-run tests and their reports.

## Reference

- [REFERENCE.md](REFERENCE.md) — the design detail behind the driver README, under the same
  section numbers: the 16 gotchas (§4), throughput and buffering (§6), open items including the
  gate-delay scanning spec (§7), and how each self-test and scan works inside (§9).
- [MANUAL_AUDIT.md](MANUAL_AUDIT.md) — every command, limit and status bit checked against
  the SR400 manual Revision 2.7, what it fixed, and a pdftotext trap that mis-pairs the
  abridged command list. The manual itself is SRS copyright and is not committed.
- [`../analysis/`](../analysis/) — studies behind the threshold advice, each reproducible from the
  real CSVs: the best level for a weak signal
  ([`low_signal_threshold/`](../analysis/low_signal_threshold/README.md)) and with an inseparable
  photon background ([`photon_background/`](../analysis/photon_background/README.md)).

## Historical record

- [HISTORY.md](HISTORY.md) — the drafts this driver came from, what was taken from them and
  what was not, two defects in the independent draft, and how the terminator disagreement was
  resolved.
- [`plans/`](plans/) — the latency-and-batching implementation plan, kept verbatim, plus the
  rebase note recording what it assumed, where each task landed, and two deliberate deviations.
  All three of its tiers are implemented.
- [`drafts/`](drafts/) — an independent early implementation of the driver, kept verbatim.
